# AI and machine learning icon credits

## Models and training

The detection-frame, document, parameter-adjustment, preference-comparison, and human-feedback symbols in `yolo.svg`, `sft.svg`, `lora-qlora.svg`, `dpo.svg`, and `rlhf.svg` are original functional illustrations created for this profile. They are not official logos of the named techniques or a particular YOLO implementation.

PNG artwork was scaled into 20-pixel icon areas and rendered with the name and gray badge background at double resolution for a 32-pixel display height. The llama.cpp SVG preserves the original paths and fills; editor metadata was removed, its local style fills were made explicit, and it was scaled into the same badge layout.
